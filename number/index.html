Number

  • Lesson 2: Indices and standard formHandbook 1.3, 1.7 · index laws · zero, negative and fractional indices · standard form
  • Lesson 3: Fractions, decimals and percentagesHandbook 1.5, 1.6, 1.12 · equivalence · ordering with < > · percentage of, increase and decrease
  • Lesson 4: Directed numbers, squares and rootsHandbook 1.3, 1.4 · add, subtract, multiply and divide negatives · temperatures and flood levels · squares, cubes and roots
  • Lesson 5: Four rules, fractions and order of operationsHandbook 1.8 · order of operations step-through · fractions with mixed numbers · decimals · negatives
  • Lesson 6: Estimating, rounding and boundsHandbook 1.9, 1.10 · decimal places and significant figures · estimating · rounding in context · upper and lower bounds
  • Lesson 7: Ratio, proportion and ratesHandbook 1.11 · sharing in a ratio · direct and inverse proportion · speed, density, pressure · map scales
  • Lesson 8: Calculator use and timeHandbook 1.13, 1.14 · efficient calculator use · sense checks · 12- and 24-hour time · durations, clocks, dials, timetables
