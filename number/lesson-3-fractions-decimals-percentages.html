Fractions, decimals and percentages

By the end of this lesson you can:
  • convert between fractions, decimals and percentages
  • order quantities using =, ≠, >, <, ≥ and ≤
  • find a percentage of a quantity, and one quantity as a percentage of another
  • calculate a percentage increase or decrease

1. Three ways to write the same amount

A fraction, a decimal and a percentage can all describe the same amount. Percent means "out of 100", so 35% is 35/100 or 0.35.

Predict first: What percentage is 3/8?
Check my prediction

37.5%. 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375, then × 100.

Choose a fraction and watch it shown three ways.

2. Converting

  • Fraction → decimal: divide the top by the bottom. 3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375
  • Decimal → percentage: multiply by 100. 0.375 = 37.5%
  • Percentage → fraction: write it over 100, then simplify. 35% = 35/100 = 7/20

Predict first: What is 35% as a fraction in its simplest form?
Check my prediction

7/20. 35/100, then divide top and bottom by 5.

Type a fraction (3/8), a decimal (0.35) or a percentage (35%).

3. Ordering with symbols

Put numbers in the same form (usually decimals) to compare them.

SymbolMeans
=equal to
≠not equal to
> <greater than, less than
≥ ≤greater than or equal to, less than or equal to

4. Percentages of quantities

Predict first: What is 25% of 240? (The calculator below starts on exactly this.)
Check my prediction

60. 25% is a quarter, and 240 ÷ 4 = 60.

Increase by 15%: multiply by 1.15 (100% + 15% = 115%).
Decrease by 15%: multiply by 0.85 (100% − 15% = 85%).
Percentage change = (change ÷ original) × 100.

A jacket costs $80. It goes up by 15%. What is the new price?

Multiplier = 1.15, so new price = 80 × 1.15 = $92.
