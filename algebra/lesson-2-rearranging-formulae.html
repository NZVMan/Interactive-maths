Rearranging formulae

By the end of this lesson you can:
  • explain what "make x the subject" means
  • rearrange a formula by doing the same thing to both sides
  • rearrange formulae that need two or more steps

1. Changing the subject

In v = u + at the letter v is the subject: it is on its own on one side. To make a different letter the subject, undo what has been done to it, one step at a time, and do the same thing to both sides so the formula stays true.

Undo operations in the reverse order to how they were built. In y = 3x + 5, x was multiplied by 3 and then 5 was added. So undo the + 5 first, then the × 3.

Opposites: + and −, × and ÷, square and square root.

Predict first: y = 3x + 5. To make x the subject, should you divide by 3 or subtract 5 first?
Check my prediction

Subtract 5 first. That gives y − 5 = 3x. Then divide everything on the left by 3: x = (y − 5)/3.

Make t the subject of v = u + at.

Subtract u from both sides: v − u = at

Divide both sides by a: (v − u)/a = t

So t = (v − u)/a

2. Two or more steps

When the letter is inside brackets, or has a power, you may need to expand, divide first, or use a square root. Take care to divide or multiply the whole side, so use brackets on the answer.
