Solving equations

By the end of this lesson you can:
  • solve one-step and two-step linear equations by doing the same to both sides
  • choose the right order for undoing operations
  • check a solution by substituting it back

1. An equation is a balance

An equation says two things are equal, like 3x + 5 = 20. To solve it, find the value of x that makes it true. Keep it balanced: whatever you do to one side, do to the other.

Undo the operations in reverse order. In 3x + 5 = 20, x was multiplied by 3, then 5 was added. So subtract 5 first, then divide by 3.

Always check your answer by putting it back into the original equation.

Predict first: To solve 3x + 5 = 20, should the first step be "divide by 3" or "subtract 5"?
Check my prediction

Subtract 5. That gives 3x = 15. Then divide by 3 to get x = 5. Check: 3 × 5 + 5 = 20 ✓.

Type an equation with one letter, or tap an example.

2. Dividing and negative x

If the letter is divided, x/4 + 3 = 9, undo the + 3 first (x/4 = 6), then multiply both sides by 4. If you get −x = 5, multiply or divide both sides by −1 to get x = −5.
