Forming equations

By the end of this lesson you can:
  • turn a word problem into an equation
  • solve the equation and check the answer makes sense in the problem
  • set up equations for perimeters, ages, costs and consecutive numbers

1. From words to an equation

1. Choose a letter for the unknown: "Let the number be x".

2. Write each part of the story as maths, in order.

3. Use "is", "equals" or "gives" to find the = sign.

4. Solve, then check against the words of the problem.

Predict first: "I think of a number, double it and add 7. The answer is 19." Which equation is right: 2x + 7 = 19 or 2(x + 7) = 19?
Check my prediction

2x + 7 = 19. You double the number first (2x), then add 7. The bracket version would add 7 first.
