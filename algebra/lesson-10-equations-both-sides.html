Unknowns on both sides and brackets

By the end of this lesson you can:
  • solve equations with the unknown on both sides
  • expand brackets before solving
  • solve equations that have negative answers or negative coefficients

1. The unknown on both sides

Get all the x terms on one side and all the numbers on the other.

Usually take away the smaller x term from both sides, so the x coefficient stays positive. Then solve like before.

Predict first: To solve 5x − 4 = 2x + 11, which x term should you remove first: 5x or 2x?
Check my prediction

Remove 2x (the smaller one) by subtracting 2x from both sides. That gives 3x − 4 = 11, so 3x = 15 and x = 5.

2. Brackets

Expand any brackets first (multiply every term inside), collect like terms, then solve. 3(x + 2) = 21 becomes 3x + 6 = 21.

Solve 2(x + 3) = x + 10.

Expand: 2x + 6 = x + 10

Subtract x: x + 6 = 10

Subtract 6: x = 4
